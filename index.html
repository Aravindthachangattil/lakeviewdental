Scared of the Dentist? Prevent Cavities & Maintain Oral Health at Home.

If you feel nervous or are scared of the dentist, the best way to minimize dental procedures is to proactively maintain oral health at home. Using a sonic electric toothbrush paired with a water flosser every night before sleep drastically reduces bacterial biofilm, stops gum disease, and helps prevent cavities before they start.

Taking medications for anxiety, depression, ADHD, high blood pressure, or undergoing cancer treatment? Many prescriptions cause severe dry mouth (xerostomia), significantly increasing your cavity risk. Explore our doctor-curated products formulated to relieve medication side effects and simplify denture care.
